What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file saved to your device by your browser when you visit a website. Sites use cookies to keep session information, remember your preferences or measure visitor behaviour. Local storage is another technology used for similar purposes, keeping data in your browser.

What this site uses

The sipsakye.com promotional website uses no cookies at all. It consists entirely of static pages and carries no technology that identifies, tracks or profiles you.

The only local record used on the site is the following:

NameTypePurpose
sy-theme Local storage Remembers your dark/light theme preference. It is kept only on your device, is never sent to any server, and contains no information that identifies you.

Why are there no advertising or analytics cookies?

This site exists only to introduce the app and provide access to the legal documents. We run no ad networks, social media trackers, heatmaps or visitor analytics tools. That is also why there is no cookie consent banner — there are no cookies for you to consent to.

If visitor analytics or a similar tool is added to the site in future, this policy will be updated and your explicit consent obtained where required.

Controlling your preference

To clear your theme preference, use your browser's option to clear site data. After clearing, the site follows your device's system theme. If you block cookies and local storage entirely in your browser settings, the site still works normally — only your theme preference is not remembered.

The mobile app

This policy covers the sipsakye.com website only. To learn how your personal data is processed in the Şipşak Ye mobile app, see the Privacy Notice and the Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated text is published on this page and the “last updated” date at the top is revised.

For questions, write to sipsakye0@gmail.com. Service provider details are on the Contact & company details page.
