Delete your account and data

You can delete your Şipşak Ye account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Use any of the routes below — options 2 and 3 do not require installing the app.

This page applies to Şipşak Ye mobile app accounts on iOS and Android.

Option 1

Delete from inside the app

The fastest route. You start the deletion yourself while signed in to your verified account.

  1. Open the Şipşak Ye app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the My Account tab in the bottom menu.
  3. In Settings, choose Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm.
  4. Confirm. Your account is closed and the deletion process begins.

Option 2 · Recommended

Delete instantly via SMS verification

No app install and no sign-in required. Verifying the code sent to your registered phone number is enough to prove your identity — deletion completes immediately.

Use international format, e.g. +905XXXXXXXXX

Your account has been deleted

Option 3

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to your registered number or are having trouble receiving SMS, you can also send your request directly by email.

Send a request email

The button below opens a draft email with the subject and body prepared. Fill in the blanks and send.

Create deletion request email

If your email client does not open

Compose the email yourself with the following:

  • To: sipsakye0@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account and data deletion request
  • Body: The email address and phone number registered to your account, and your full name

Identity verification: To make sure a request does not affect someone else's account, we need to confirm that it really comes from the account holder. We may contact you on the registered phone number or ask you to confirm details of your most recent order. Deletion does not start before verification is complete.

What is deleted

Deleted and retained data

Data that is deleted

  • Account and profile details (name, username)
  • Contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Your favourites list
  • Notifications and saved device/notification permissions
  • Search history and cart
  • All active sessions on your account (you're signed out everywhere)

Data retained by law

Some records must be kept after a deletion request, as required by legislation. These records are made independent of your identity — your name, phone number and email are not displayed alongside them:

  • Invoice, payment and accounting records (tax and commercial law)
  • Order, delivery, rating and review records (consumer law, potential disputes, and abuse prevention)
  • Records showing when you gave and withdrew consent (required as proof under KVKK)
  • Transaction security and access log records

These records are used only for the legal purpose that requires them to be kept, and are deleted, destroyed or anonymised once the retention period ends.

Timing: Verified deletion requests are processed as soon as possible. Records that must be retained are kept until the end of the periods set out in the relevant legislation. Retention principles are explained in our Privacy Policy.

Before you delete

Things to know

What if I have an order in progress?

If you have an active order, deletion starts once that order is complete. The data needed to deliver your order continues to be processed until then.

Can I get my account back after deleting it?

No. Once deletion is complete, your account, order history, saved addresses and any coupon or campaign entitlements cannot be restored. To order again you would need to create a new account.

Can I delete only certain data and keep my account?

Yes. Under KVKK you can also request the erasure or correction of specific data. Send your request to sipsakye0@gmail.com. Your rights are listed in the Privacy Notice.

Does uninstalling the app delete my account?

No. Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account. To delete it, use one of the routes above.

Your right to terminate membership, and Şipşak Ye's right to close or suspend accounts, are set out in the Terms of Use. Questions? Write to sipsakye0@gmail.com.
