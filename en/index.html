Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Which cities does Şipşak Ye serve?

Our service areas keep expanding. Once you enter your address in the app, you immediately see the restaurants and markets you can order from in your area.

Does the app cost anything?

Downloading and using the app is free. When you order, you pay only for the products and, where applicable, the delivery fee — all amounts are shown clearly in the basket before payment.

How can I pay?

Right now only payment on delivery is available: cash or card at the door. Online payment isn't offered yet — we're planning to add it.

What if my order is late or something goes wrong?

Reach us from the support section in the app or at sipsakye0@gmail.com. Full cancellation, exchange and refund conditions are set out in our Terms of Use.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on our account deletion page. You can submit the request without installing the app.

How is my personal data protected?

Your personal data is processed in line with Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). See our Privacy Policy and Privacy Notice for details.
