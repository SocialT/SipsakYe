Subject and nature of the goods/services

Şipşak Ye is a digital platform acting as an intermediary between users and member businesses, enabling users to order food, grocery and similar products online for delivery. The name, quantity, characteristics, unit price and any variations (portion, packaging, etc.) of each item are shown to the user on the member business's product page and on the order summary screen; this form sets out the general framework of that information.

Total price and payment

Before an order is confirmed, the item prices, any delivery/service fee, applied discounts or coupons, and the total amount payable (including VAT) are shown separately on the order summary screen. You can pay on delivery (cash or card) or, where offered, by online payment. Payment methods and any additional fees are described in detail in the Terms of Use.

Delivery

Orders are delivered to the address specified at checkout, through the member business's or Şipşak Ye's contracted courier/delivery service. The estimated delivery time shown at checkout is not a binding commitment and may vary with order volume, region or unforeseen disruptions. See Terms of Use — Delivery for details.

Right of withdrawal

Under Turkish consumer law (Law No. 6502 and Article 15 of the Distance Contracts Regulation), goods that are perishable, have a limited shelf life, or whose protective packaging, seal or wrapping has been opened after delivery for health and hygiene reasons — including food orders, grocery, fresh-produce and beverage items — are exempt from the right of withdrawal. Most items offered through the Şipşak Ye Platform fall within this exemption. You may cancel free of charge before confirming your order; after confirmation, if preparation has not yet started, you may request cancellation via sipsakye0@gmail.com. Even where the right of withdrawal does not apply, your rights regarding defective, missing or incorrectly delivered products remain unaffected. See Terms of Use — Right of withdrawal for the full provision.

Complaints and dispute resolution

Any complaint or request regarding your order, delivery, payment or refund can be sent to sipsakye0@gmail.com. In consumer disputes, you may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee or Consumer Court at your place of residence, within the monetary limits set annually by the Ministry of Trade.
