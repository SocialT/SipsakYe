Privacy Notice (KVKK)

Information provided under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data about which of your personal data is processed, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and with whom it may be shared.

Last updated: 13 August 2026 · Data controller: the Şipşak Ye Platform

This is an informative English summary. The legally binding version is the Turkish text, published at Aydınlatma Metni. In the event of any discrepancy, the Turkish text prevails.

Personal data collected

  • Membership (profile) data — name and surname, date of birth (if you provide it)
  • Contact data — phone number, address, email address
  • Location data — the location you mark in the app or activate on your device
  • Customer transaction data — usage records, instructions and requests, favourite restaurants and cuisines, order ID, date of last information change, ratings, reviews and notes on restaurants and orders, membership date, campaigns used
  • Transaction security data — IP address, login credentials, logging of resources accessed by suppliers during support, and wallet-specific actions such as password creation and reset
  • Financial data — information, documents and payment records showing financial outcomes, and the card number in masked form (first four and last six digits)
  • Visual and audio records — live help or phone call records relating to an order, and email correspondence
  • Legal transaction data — data processed to establish and follow up legal claims and obligations, and for compliance
  • Marketing data — discounts and campaigns used, order timing, favourite products, coupon information, which app you registered or ordered through, shopping habits, and the reports and assessments produced from them
  • Risk management data — information collected to protect the platform's commercial reputation

Special categories of personal data are not collected or processed. You are responsible for not sharing special category data through any point of interaction with the platform.

Purposes of processing

Your data is processed for the following purposes, limited and proportionate to them:

  • Performing the contract: delivering your order, performing the contract you have entered into with the platform, and following up its other processes
  • Improving user experience and personalisation: planning satisfaction activities, market research, marketing processes, push notifications, profiling and analysis, personalised discounts and content, digital marketing and remarketing
  • Providing products and services: sales processes, after-sales support, customer relationship management, follow-up of requests and complaints, and follow-up of contractual or legal claims
  • Legal, technical and commercial security: audit activities, operational compliance with legislation and procedures, security of operations, keeping data accurate and up to date, and providing information to authorised institutions
  • Business operations: information security, IT infrastructure, finance and accounting, legal affairs, efficiency analysis, corporate communications, logistics, production and operations, and prevention of fraudulent activity
  • Commercial strategy: managing relationships with business partners and suppliers, strategic planning, supply chain management, and performance management

Method and legal basis of collection

Your personal data is collected electronically through email, the relevant websites and the mobile application, phone, social media accounts you allow the platform to access, and the call centre, as well as physically by post or courier.

Processing is based on the following legal grounds set out in KVKK:

  • Express provision in the law
  • Necessity for the establishment or performance of a contract between you and us
  • Necessity for compliance with our legal obligations
  • Data having been made public by you (to the extent compatible with your purpose in doing so)
  • Necessity for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right
  • Necessity for our legitimate interests, provided this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms

Who your data may be shared with

Your data may be shared with our affiliates, shareholders, business partners, and authorised public institutions and private law persons in Turkey. Profile, contact, location, customer transaction, transaction security, risk management and marketing data may, to a limited extent, also be shared with affiliates, shareholders and business partners abroad, in accordance with the relevant provisions of KVKK.

Your rights

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information if it has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom your data has been transferred
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, and that this be notified to third parties
  • Request erasure or destruction where the grounds for processing have ceased to exist, and that this be notified to third parties
  • Object to a result against you produced solely by automated analysis
  • Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing

How to apply

Send your requests to sipsakye0@gmail.com or through any of the platform's contact channels. Applications that do not meet the conditions set by the Personal Data Protection Authority will not be assessed until any additional information we request from you is completed.

Requests are concluded as quickly as possible and within the maximum operational period for the service. If responding to your request incurs an additional cost, the fee set out in the tariff determined by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged.

Şipşak Ye reserves the right to update this notice unilaterally where business or legal requirements arise.
