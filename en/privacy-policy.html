Personal Data Protection, Processing and Privacy Policy

How the Şipşak Ye Platform processes, stores, transfers, protects and erases personal data under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK).

Processing principles

Şipşak Ye processes personal data either on the legal grounds set out in the legislation or with the explicit consent of the data subject, and always in line with the following principles:

  • Processing in compliance with the law and the rule of good faith
  • Ensuring that personal data is accurate and, where necessary, up to date
  • Processing for specific, explicit and legitimate purposes
  • Being relevant, limited and proportionate to the purposes of processing
  • Retaining data only for the period stipulated in legislation or required for the purpose

Şipşak Ye does not collect or process special categories of personal data (such as race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief, religion, health, sexual life, criminal convictions, biometric or genetic data).

Data categories

The following categories of personal data may be processed:

  • Profile data — username, name and surname, date of birth (if provided)
  • Contact data — phone number, address, email address, IP address
  • Location data — the location you select or activate on your device while using the platform
  • Customer transaction data — order records, favourite restaurants and cuisines, order IDs, ratings, reviews and notes, membership date, campaign usage
  • Transaction security data — IP address, login credentials, access logs, wallet-related actions such as password reset
  • Financial data — payment records and, in masked form, the first six and last four digits of the card number
  • Visual and audio records — live help or phone call records relating to an order, and email correspondence
  • Legal transaction data — data processed for the establishment and follow-up of legal claims and obligations
  • Marketing data — usage habits, preferences, discounts and campaigns used, order timing, favourite products, coupon information, and the reports produced from them
  • Risk management data — information collected to protect the commercial reputation of the platform

Purposes of processing

Personal data is processed for purposes including, and limited to:

  • Delivering your order and performing the contract between you and the platform
  • Planning and carrying out customer relationship management, satisfaction activities, and the follow-up of requests and complaints
  • Planning and executing sales, marketing and after-sales support processes, including personalised campaigns, push notifications and targeting
  • Planning and running logistics, production, operational and business continuity activities
  • Managing relationships with restaurants, business partners and suppliers
  • Establishing and managing information security processes and IT infrastructure
  • Following up finance, accounting and legal matters
  • Ensuring the security of platform operations and preventing fraudulent activity
  • Providing information to authorised public bodies where required by legislation

Transfer to third parties

Personal data may be transferred, limited to the purpose in each case, to:

  • Restaurants and member businesses, for the fulfilment of your order
  • Suppliers providing outsourced services necessary for the platform's operations
  • Business partners, in anonymised form (separate explicit consent is obtained where further transfer is needed)
  • Affiliates and shareholders, limited to the conduct and audit of commercial activities
  • Authorised public institutions and private law persons, limited to the purpose of their lawful request

Transfers take place on the legal grounds listed in KVKK — including explicit consent, an express provision in legislation, necessity for the performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, the establishment or protection of a right, and legitimate interests that do not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.

Security measures

Şipşak Ye takes reasonable technical and administrative measures — taking technological possibilities and implementation cost into account — to process personal data lawfully, store it in secure environments, and prevent unauthorised access, accidental loss, deliberate damage and unauthorised erasure. These include installing and operating firewall software and hardware, and establishing technical security systems for storage areas with lawful backup programmes.

In accordance with Article 12 of KVKK, if it is established that personal data has been obtained by others through unlawful means, Şipşak Ye notifies the affected data subject and the Personal Data Protection Board as soon as possible.

Your rights

As a data subject you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information if it has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom your data has been transferred
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, and that this be notified to third parties
  • Request erasure or destruction where the reasons for processing no longer exist, and that this be notified to third parties
  • Object to a result against you arising from analysis carried out solely by automated systems
  • Claim compensation for damage arising from unlawful processing

These rights cannot be asserted in the exceptional cases listed in Article 28 of KVKK.

Retention and erasure

Personal data is retained for the periods stipulated in the relevant legislation and in line with legal obligations. Where legislation sets no period, data is processed for as long as the related activity requires under the platform's practices and commercial custom, and is then deleted, destroyed or anonymised. Data may be kept beyond that point only to serve as evidence in a potential legal dispute or to establish a defence or assert a related right.

Contact

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, write to sipsakye0@gmail.com. For identity verification we may contact you on your registered phone number or ask you to confirm details of your most recent order. Requests made on behalf of a data subject by a third party require a special power of attorney issued through a notary.

If your application is rejected, the response is found insufficient, or no response is given within the statutory period, you may apply to the Personal Data Protection Board within thirty days of learning the response and in any case within sixty days of the application date.
