Orders

Orders, delivery and refunds

My order is late — what should I do?

Delivery times shown in the app are estimates and can vary with regional demand and issues at the business. Where delivery exceeds 60 minutes from the moment the order was placed, you may request a refund without accepting the delivery.

See the Terms of Use summary, or the binding Turkish text under Ücret İadesi, İptal ve Değişim Süreçleri.

I received a wrong or damaged item

For grocery items you may refuse delivery if a product is found to be past its expiry date or its packaging is torn. If a wrong item is delivered, it is exchanged for the correct one.

It matters that you raise this at the moment of delivery. Full conditions are in the Terms of Use.

Can I cancel my order?

If the order has not yet moved to the preparation stage, you can submit a cancellation request through the app or our support channels. For orders already in preparation, cancellation and refund conditions in the Terms of Use apply.

When will my refund arrive?

Refunds are issued to the same channel the payment came from. For card payments, the time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank.

Account and payment

Your account and payments

I forgot my password

Use the “Forgot password” link on the sign-in screen. We send a link to your registered email address so you can set a new password.

I want to change my phone number

Each account can have one verified phone number. You can change the number registered to your account from within the app by verifying again.

Which payment methods can I use?

Online credit/debit card, card at the door and cash on delivery. Available options vary by business and are shown during checkout.

Where are my card details stored?

If you consent to saving your card details for faster ordering, they are stored in the infrastructure of a third-party payment service provider.

Reporting

Reporting inappropriate content and abuse

Users can post reviews and ratings on Şipşak Ye. If you come across content that breaks the rules, report it and we will review it and take action.

  1. Report from within the app Use the report option next to the review in question.
  2. Report by email Write to sipsakye0@gmail.com with “Content report” in the subject line. Please include a screenshot of the content, and your order number and date if relevant.
  3. Review We assess reports as quickly as we can. Content found to break the rules may be removed; repeated violations may lead to the account being restricted, suspended or closed.

Rules on user content and Şipşak Ye's right to intervene are set out in the Terms of Use.
