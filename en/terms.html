Parties and subject

The agreement is between (a) the website sipsakye.com and the Şipşak Ye Platform mobile application, and (b) the natural person who becomes a member and places an order for goods or services for non-commercial, non-professional purposes (the “User”). These terms also apply, to the extent appropriate, to orders placed without membership.

Şipşak Ye is a digital platform acting as an intermediary service provider between member businesses and users, offering food, grocery and similar products. It may also supply its own products and services, and provides services from businesses belonging to the company it is affiliated with.

Membership and account

You can become a member by completing the sign-up form or through the supported social media or Apple accounts. Şipşak Ye may request additional information or documents to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, and reserves the right to verify you with a one-time password (OTP) via SMS or messaging platforms.

Users confirm that they are over 18, or that if under 18 they use the platform with the approval of their parent or legal guardian.

Registration requires an email address and a password of your choosing. Your password is known only to you; setting and protecting it is entirely your responsibility. Şipşak Ye assumes that whoever signs in with a registered email and password is the user under this agreement, and has no obligation to investigate the source or authority of sign-ins.

Şipşak Ye may share your registered name, address and phone number with the member business fulfilling the order and with the delivery service, solely for correct delivery of the order.

User obligations

When using the platform, you undertake among other things to:

  • Provide accurate information; one verified phone number may be registered per account, which you can change through re-verification
  • Take personal responsibility for all transactions carried out with your registered phone number
  • Not access the services in ways other than those determined by Şipşak Ye, and not modify the software
  • Not request or obtain tobacco products or alcoholic drinks through the platform, whose sale is not carried out under the applicable regulations
  • Not harass or threaten other users, and not behave in ways that negatively affect their use of the platform
  • Not publish defamatory, immoral, unlawful or inappropriate material, and not use such usernames, photos or nicknames
  • Not advertise, sell goods or services, or run surveys, competitions or chain-letter activity
  • Not engage in activity that obstructs the service for others (spam, viruses, trojans and similar)
  • Not use the platform's services for commercial or advertising purposes without permission

Where these rules are breached, Şipşak Ye may intervene, remove the user from the service and terminate their membership.

The platform's rights

Şipşak Ye may close, suspend or restrict an account where it establishes that the account is being used by a third party, that it is being misused, or that there has been conduct contrary to law, morality or these terms. You may request termination of your membership at any time — see Delete your account.

Coupons, discounts and promotions

Any coupon defined in the process is single-use, cannot be divided, combined with other coupons, converted into cash or sold for money.

Payment

You can pay for your order using the methods offered by the relevant member business: payment on delivery, or, where available, instant online payment by debit card, credit card or a similar payment instrument.

Where a payment made through an online payment method is used unlawfully by someone other than the cardholder, the matter is handled under Law No. 5464 on Bank Cards and Credit Cards and the related regulation, and Şipşak Ye cannot be held liable.

Şipşak Ye may be exclusively authorised to collect the price of ordered products from users on behalf of and for the account of the member business. It reserves the right to add new payment methods or remove existing ones at any time.

If you consent to your card details being saved for faster ordering, those details are stored in the infrastructure of third-party payment service providers.

Delivery

If you are not present at the address you selected — including where you have given an incorrect address — your order will not be left at another address.

Where Priority Delivery is offered and you select it, you accept the additional fee set by Şipşak Ye. Priority Delivery is a specialised service in which the courier brings your order directly to you without extra stops. It generally aims to shorten delivery time, but this cannot be guaranteed and stated delivery times are estimates only. Şipşak Ye reserves the right to change the fees related to this service, and to pause, update or cancel it at any time.

Cancellation, exchange and refunds

Şipşak Ye reserves the right not to issue a refund for reasons arising solely from the user — for example, not being at home, giving an incorrect address, or not accepting the delivery. As an intermediary service provider, it accepts no liability in disputes arising from the return conditions applied by member businesses.

Where requested at the moment of delivery, exchange, cancellation or refund is provided in the following cases:

  • Grocery items: the delivery may be refused if a product is found to be past its expiry date or its packaging is torn. If a wrong item is delivered, only that item is exchanged for the correct one.
  • Fresh produce: only items that are biologically spoiled to the point of being unusable may be refused. By their nature, agricultural products may not look exactly like the image in the app, and an order cannot be cancelled on the basis of appearance alone.
  • Cold-chain products such as ice cream: relevant conditions at the moment of delivery apply.

For food orders that have already moved into preparation and have been paid for in advance, the platform or the business reserves the right to decide whether to issue a refund.

Refunds are issued only to the same channel through which payment was made; for card payments the time taken depends on that channel's processes.

Delivery times shown on the platform are estimated ranges, not firm commitments, and may vary with regional order density and disruptions at the business or on the platform. Where delivery exceeds 60 minutes from the moment the order was received, a refund may be requested without accepting the product. Refunds, coupon usage and product exchanges are carried out only at the customer's request.
